Pair Phone

Gesture Guide

Every gesture GestureLink supports, organised by mode

Quick Setup
  1. Make sure your phone and this PC are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  2. Click Pair Phone on the Dashboard & scan the QR code with your phone camera.
  3. Enter the 6-digit PIN shown in the sidebar on your phone when asked.
  4. Tap Allow on the approval popup that appears on this screen.
  5. Open the GestureLink controller on your phone — you’re ready!
MODE 1   Cursor Mode — Camera Hand Gestures

Point your phone camera at your hand. GestureLink reads your finger positions to control the mouse.

☝️
Index Finger Only
Move cursor — index tip steers the pointer
✌️
Index + Middle (V)
Left click & drag — hold to drag files/windows
🤘
Rock Sign
Right click — opens context menus
🌡️
3 Fingers Up
Scroll — move hand up/down to scroll the page
🤙
Pinky Hold (1 sec)
Switch mode → Meet Paint (Premium)
💡 Tip: Keep your hand well-lit and within the camera frame. A comfortable distance is 30–50 cm from the camera.
MODE 2   Meet Paint Mode PREMIUM

Draws transparent ink strokes directly on your screen — visible over Google Meet, Zoom, and any other app. Great for presentations and teaching.

☝️
Index Only
Laser pointer dot — no ink drawn
✌️
Index + Middle (V)
Draw ink stroke on screen
🤘
Rock Sign
Erase the nearest ink stroke
🌡️
3 Fingers (Hold 2s)
Clear all drawings from screen
🤙
Pinky Hold (1 sec)
Exit Meet Paint → back to Cursor Mode
💡 Pro Tip: Use a USB webcam for GestureLink and your PC’s built-in camera for the video call — so your hand doesn’t appear in the call!
MODE 3   Builder Mode — 3D Isometric Scene PREMIUM

Build isometric 3D scenes with your hands. Place, move, and erase voxel cubes using single and two-hand gestures.

☝️
Index Only (Pointing)
Ghost preview — shows where the cube will be placed
✌️
Index + Middle (V)
Place cube — hold briefly to paint multiple cubes
🤏
Thumb + Index Pinch
Pick up & move a cube
🌡️
3 Fingers Up
Erase nearest cube
🤘
Rock Sign
Undo last action
✊
Left Hand Fist
Two-hand mode: rotate & zoom scene
🤙
Pinky Hold (1 sec)
Exit Builder → back to Cursor Mode
Controlling Another PC (Agent Mode)
  1. On the target PC, run GestureLink_Agent.exe.
  2. Both PCs must be on the same Wi-Fi (or VPN).
  3. Go to the Devices tab — the PC appears under Nearby PCs.
  4. Click Connect and control it from your phone exactly like your own PC.
💡 Corporate network? Enter the target PC’s IP address manually in the Devices tab. Use the Fix Firewall button if the PC is not discoverable.
